AI and product design work in development

Five products, each started from a problem we think deserves attention: online assessments, personal finance, small business commerce, design education and Islamic knowledge. All five are in development, and names and details may change.

CommerceIn development

Asaan Bazaar

Helping small businesses reach more customers.

Small businesses can have a strong product and still struggle to be found by buyers. Asaan Bazaar is being developed so makers can focus on what they make, while we work towards an easier path from product to customer.

Islamic knowledge and AIIn development

Mizan

AI for exploring Islamic knowledge, built with care.

Some Muslims hesitate to rely on general-purpose AI for religious questions, given concerns about trust and the reliability of knowledge. Mizan is an AI research project exploring how technology can support understanding of Islamic teachings, history and scholarship, grounded in Islamic principles, with consent and the prevention of harm at its centre.

EducationIn development

Exam Guard

Fairer online assessments.

Online assessment tools make testing convenient, but educators still face questions of fairness and accountability. Exam Guard is being developed to help teachers run more accountable assessments while giving students a fair environment to show what they know.

Personal financeIn development

Hisaab Kitab

Understand the money moving through your life.

Records of frequent transactions end up scattered across financial channels, and a list is not the same as a clear picture. Hisaab Kitab is being developed to turn everyday financial activity into a clearer view of personal spending, so people can recognise patterns and make informed decisions.

Design educationIn development

DesignPlay

Design ideas you can experience, not just read.

Design concepts can be hard to reach when taught through long theory or unfamiliar language, especially for learners who study best in Urdu. DesignPlay is an interactive learning project exploring how playful experiences and visual explanations can make complex design ideas easier to grasp, with underserved learners in mind.

Our AI and product design process

01

Understand the people and the business

Every project starts with the people who live with the problem and the business that has to support the answer. What people say first is often the easiest answer to give, not the full picture. We keep asking until we reach the need underneath.

02

Research the problem in depth

One method shows one side of a problem. We combine three, so what people do, what they tell us and how widely a pattern holds can be checked against each other.

03

Bring the right disciplines together

Each discipline sees a different part of the same problem. Design asks what people need, technology asks what can be built, business asks what can last. We bring them into the work from the first day, so every idea is tested against the others.

04

Design the solution that fits

The solution is assembled from the parts we studied: the needs we found, the evidence we gathered and the disciplines that shaped it. It then goes back to the people it is for, and changes wherever they struggle.
